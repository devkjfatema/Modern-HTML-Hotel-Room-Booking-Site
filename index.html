Welcome to Your Guesthouse

We are a small guesthouse with only a handful of rooms, set around a garden courtyard and a quiet pool. It feels more like staying with friends than checking into a hotel.

Mornings begin with breakfast on the terrace; afternoons are for the pool, a book or the town nearby. Ask us anything — we live here and love sharing it.
